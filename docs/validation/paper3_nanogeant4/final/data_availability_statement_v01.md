# Data Availability Statement

The benchmark study files, raw scorer outputs, derived CSV tables, manuscript source, and figure-generation scripts that support this study are available within the ShieldLab G4 project package prepared for submission.

For the present paper, the principal benchmark outputs and derived figure inputs are archived under `results/paper3/`, and the manuscript-specific assets are archived under `docs/validation/paper3_nanogeant4/`.

The software used to generate the benchmark results is distributed as ShieldLab G4 v1.1 under the MIT licence. The paper-specific data package is released under CC-BY 4.0. Additional derived materials can be provided by the corresponding author on reasonable request.
