# Highlights

- Benchmarked three Geant4 regimes for HDPE/Bi2O3 nanocomposite shielding.
- Effective-medium transport matched XCOM with 0.549% mean error at 30 wt%.
- Regimes A, B, and C agreed within 3% at 30-150 keV for matched composition.
- G4PVParameterised handled a 1 um RVE with 2,888 explicit nanoparticles.
- G4MultiUnion was practical only up to about 45 spheres on 8 GB hardware.
